## English
App name: Noor
Brief: Prayer times, azkar and Quran
Full introduction:
Noor is a simple, calm Islamic app, built as a sadaqah jariyah (ongoing charity) for the late parents of its developer.

- Prayer times for your city or location, with several calculation methods
- Adhan and iqamah notifications that work with the screen locked, several adhan voices to choose from, and an iqamah delay you set per prayer
- The dua after the adhan, with its virtue
- The full Hisn al-Muslim: 132 chapters of azkar and duas, organized and searchable
- Morning, evening, after-prayer, sleep and waking azkar and more
- Virtues of the surahs from authentic hadith, with sources
- The full Quran text working offline, with a bookmark and khatmah tracker
- Recitations by many reciters, downloadable for offline listening
- Qibla direction, tasbeeh counter, Hijri calendar and occasions, zakat calculator
- Three app skins. No ads, no account, no tracking

We collect no data about you.
New features: First release.
